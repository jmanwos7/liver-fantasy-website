We still have 10 teams after Week 10 that are a win or loss away from the playoff picture. Some pretty key games happened this week as Porch’s fall from grace continues, losing 4 straight in a 5-4 matchup with Ben. Aidan lost his win-and-in-game to Grecko this week as both managers would have moved to 5-5 if the outcome was different. Ridilla and Danny both win their much-needed bye weeks, making this the best playoff race in the league’s 5-year history! Row up to the Playoffs!

Rowan

🔥

Two Words. One Man. Fits the description of someone who would commit a felony. Spanky’s number player gooned to all-time.
